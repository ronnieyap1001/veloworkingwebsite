How we deliver

Five steps. Same flow, every project.

Whether it is one enclosure or a full multi-category production package, the engagement runs the same way — so you always know where the project stands.

01

Scope & capture

We meet, walk the requirement, and translate drawings, specs, and quantities into a clean production brief — owned by us, not passed raw to vendors.

02

Vendor selection

The right vendor is engaged for quotation. You receive one consolidated, client-facing proposal — not three quotes to compare.

03

Production management

Once confirmed, we manage the schedule end-to-end — material sourcing, production milestones, and factory-level QA where it matters.

04

Quality assurance

Before shipment, items are inspected against the agreed specification. We hold the vendor to account on non-conformance — not you.

05

Delivery & handover

Hardware is delivered to your site or warehouse with full documentation. Repeat-order schedules available for ongoing requirements.
