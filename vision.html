Founder's note · Our vision

Why I started Velo Working.

A decade inside companies. The same pattern, repeating. A systems problem disguised as a people problem.

The short version

I spent over a decade inside companies, watching the same pattern repeat. Capability becomes a burden — the more capable you are, the more you carry that isn't yours. Resources came to me. Politics came to me. My actual scope shifted, became the culture, and quietly cost me my performance and my promotion.

At the top, no one knew what was happening on the floor. Decisions ran on words, not numbers. The data didn't reach the room. Whoever was closest to the boss got heard. Whoever told the better story got believed.

Competent people left, one at a time. The ones who stayed learned to dig emails for accountability. Every conversation had to be black-and-white. The hardworking became the careful, and the careful stopped doing real work.

It is a systems problem disguised as a people problem. Not better leadership. Not more training. A system that captures what actually happened, in numbers, the moment it happened — so decisions stop running on memory and politics.

Existing systems don't reach SMEs. The licences are heavy, the implementation heavier, and the implementer rarely understands how an SME actually runs. So I built Velo Working: no-code operational systems for SMEs. Vendor-neutral. Fixed scope. Fixed fee. Your team owns it after I leave. The same systems the big companies have, sized for an operation that can't afford a six-figure rollout — so nobody on your team has to become careful before they can do real work again.

Our vision

Real work. Restored.

An operating reality where capability is rewarded, not punished. Where the floor and the boardroom share the same numbers. Where SMEs run on the same caliber of system the giants do — without the licence trap or the five-year contract.

That is what Velo Working is here to build, one company at a time.

Ronnie Yap · Founder & Director · PMP
