Questions

WSH reporting, answered.

Is this a WSH hazard reporting app?

Yes. It's built for exactly that: any worker reports a hazard from a phone in under a minute — photo, location and description — and the system routes it to the right owner with a deadline. Every hazard is tracked from report to verified closure, giving you the record you need under the Workplace Safety and Health (WSH) Act.

Does it support WSH incident reporting to MOM?

It does. Incidents, injuries and near-misses are logged with a full audit trail — dates, people, medical and follow-up records — so the information you need to notify the Ministry of Manpower (MOM) under the WSH (Incident Reporting) Regulations is captured in one place rather than pieced together after the fact. Can it run scheduled safety inspections?

Yes — it's also a safety inspection app. Recurring inspections open on schedule and assign themselves out; anything that fails a checklist becomes a repair job with an owner and a due date. Nobody has to remember, and nothing quietly slips.

Is it suitable for construction, manufacturing and facilities SMEs?

That's who it's built for. Whether you run a construction site, a plant, or facilities across several locations, workers report from the field on a phone and management sees every hazard, inspection and incident on one dashboard — no weekend spent compiling reports.

Is worker and injury data handled in line with the PDPA?

Your system runs on infrastructure independently certified to international security and privacy standards (SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR), which supports handling worker and injury records in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). The platform account stays in your company's name.

Can the safety system connect to the rest of our operations?

Yes. Safety is one module in a wider custom operations system — you can add inventory, job tracking, approvals and more when you're ready, all on the same platform. Start with hazard reporting and grow from there.
