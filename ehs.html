The alternative

Group chats are where hazards go quiet.

Today the sling photo lands in a group chat, the follow-up lives on a clipboard, and the closure — if it happened — is in someone's head. When the auditor or MOM asks for records, you reconstruct the story from screenshots. That is not a safety system. It is a memory test your company sits for every day.

The operating principle

Reported is not closed.

Every module runs the same five stages. They are the stage tags you saw on the timeline above.

01

Report

Anyone, from a phone: photo, pinned location, category. Under a minute.

02

Route

Scored by likelihood × exposure × consequence, assigned to a named owner with a deadline. Nobody forwards an email.

03

Rectify

Tracked to done. Evidence is required, not optional.

04

Verify

A second person confirms the fix on site. Desk close-outs don't count.

05

Close

On the record, on the dashboard, ready for the auditor.
