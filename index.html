Common questions

Answers, straight.

How do I get a custom app without hiring a developer?

You tell me the problem; I build the app on a no-code platform — faster and far cheaper than custom code, and you keep full ownership.

How is this different from buying an ERP?

An ERP makes you fit its way of working. This is built around how your company already runs, and you only build the parts you need.

What if I only have one specific problem?

That's the ideal starting point. We solve the one thing that hurts most, then add more when it makes sense.

What happens if we stop working together?

The system is documented and built to run without me. No lock-in — it's yours to keep and hand to anyone.
