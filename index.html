The problem

You didn't fail the system. The system failed you.

Three operational failures recur in almost every SME we walk into — and they compound silently until the damage is impossible to ignore.

01

You can't hold onto what you've learned.

Knowledge lives in one person's head, one WhatsApp thread, or one spreadsheet only the "wizard" understands. When they leave, so does the company's memory.

02

Your staff spend their days copying data.

A 30-person SME typically loses 1–1.5 full-time headcount to pure duplicate data entry. Salary paid for work that creates zero new information.

03

The numbers don't add up.

What goes in is wrong, so what comes out is unreliable. Decisions made on gut feel because reports can't be trusted. AI? Forget it — AI needs clean structured data, and you don't have any.

Why it matters

Every day the system stays broken, you're paying twice.

Once for the manpower to run it manually. Once for the competitors compounding ahead of you.

How we work

Diagnose. Implement. Hand it back.

Ninety days from first meeting to your team running the system themselves. Fixed scope. Fixed fee. Written in plain English before we start.

01
Weeks 1–2 · Diagnose

Walk the floor. Quantify the cost.

A two-week audit of your current operations. We watch how work actually flows and quantify the hidden cost of the status quo. You get a written report — honest, not a sales pitch — with a fixed-scope, fixed-price proposal attached.

02
Weeks 3–12 · Implement

Build while the business keeps running.

A focused sprint on the modules that matter most. Your staff learn the system as we build it, not after we leave. We configure on Jodoo, migrate data, integrate with the tools you already use, and go live in increments.

03
Week 13+ · Hand back

You own it. We stay reachable.

Documentation your team can read. Two staff trained to run it. You own the software, data, server, workflows. We stay reachable for task-based support — you pay when there's work, nothing when there isn't. No retainer trap.

Manifesto

Why Velo Working exists.

Singapore and Malaysia run on SMEs. They are not a secondary economy — they are the economy.

99% of enterprises in Singapore. 70% of the workforce. Nearly half of GDP. These are the precision machinists supplying global semiconductor lines, the family stamping shops that have exported to Japan for thirty years, the second-generation traders digitising businesses their parents built by hand.

But the ground is shifting. AI-ready competitors compound their advantages every year. SMEs still running on WhatsApp, email, and spreadsheets are not falling behind linearly — they are falling behind exponentially.

We built Velo Working to close that gap — with fair pricing, real engineering understanding, and the accountability to stay until it works.
