What your system does

Four simple things, done properly.

01 / 04

Capture all your data

Every job, order, item and form lives in one place. No more chasing numbers across spreadsheets, chat threads and paper.

02 / 04

Update it from anywhere

Your team updates the system from the floor, the site or the road — on a phone. The record is current the moment work happens.

03 / 04

Work together, live

Everyone sees the same up-to-date information at once. No emailing files back and forth, no “which version is this?”

04 / 04

Automate your approvals

Requests route to the right person, send their own reminders and record every sign-off. The workflow runs without you chasing it.
